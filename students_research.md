<h2><font color="cyan">For Prospective Students, Interns, Project Assistants, and Postdoctoral Researchers</font></h2>

I receive a large number of inquiries every day from students and researchers seeking opportunities to work with me in various capacities. Unfortunately, it is not possible for me to respond to every email individually.

Please read the following guidelines carefully before contacting me:

- **Online degrees and certifications:** I do not consider online degrees or certifications equivalent to regular, in-person academic degrees for recruitment or academic opportunities in my group. If your qualifications consist exclusively of online degrees or certifications, please do not email me regarding opportunities to work with me.

- **Online degree programmes:** I do not encourage students pursuing online degree programmes to seek research or internship opportunities with me as part of their programme requirements.

- **Interns:** I do not have bandwidth to support short-term interns unless you are willing to work for couple of years.

- **Potential Ph.D. students:** If you are an undergraduate/graduate student pursuing or holding a regular, in-person degree from a reputed college, university, or academic institution, you are welcome to email me regarding potential Ph.D opportunities. I strongly encourage you apply for funding via **INSPIRE, UGC, CSIR etc**.

- **Postdoctoral researchers:** If you are interested in a postdoctoral position, it is strongly encouraged that you have your own fellowship or funding secured, or are eligible to obtain independent funding through schemes such as **ANRF, NBHM, or other funding agencies**, before contacting me regarding postdoctoral opportunities.

Given the volume of inquiries I receive, please ensure that you meet the relevant criteria before emailing me. I appreciate your understanding.


<h2><font color="cyan">For students interested in working with me</font></h2>

Some points for students to consider before deciding to work with me:

<li> You will need to invest considerable amount of time (minimum of 1 year).</li>
<li> You will need to have a problem solving mentality.</li>
<li> You will need to have some exposure to programming or at least be willing to learn programming. Even if your work is completely theoretical, I want the students to know how to program. </li>
<li> Don't expect quick fix "research".</li>
<li> Do not expect to get research done by simply running canned software or AI agents.</li>
<li> Don't expect me to hand hold you. You are in-charge of the project. I am only a wall to bounce ideas and to guide you. You will have to do the work.</li>
<li> You will need to take care of the administrative stuff, like scheduling your talk, figuring out how to submit your thesis, etc. You may contact your department office and they will guide you.</li>
<li> Last but probably the most important point; You should communicate (both written and oral) clearly in English. I am not a psychologist; hence I am not capable of reading your mind.</li>

<br>

<h2><font color="cyan">For students approaching me for recommendation letters</font></h2>

I provide recommendation only to students who have done research work with me for a considerable period of time. Though I welcome you to take my class, I am no longer writing reference letters for students who have attended my class(es).
